### 2. Week 12 - Demographic Data Analysis
- **Description:** Conducted a comprehensive exploratory and inferential analysis on a demographic dataset of 1,600+ respondents. The study examined distributions, identified outliers, and explored relationships between demographic and behavioral variables.  
- **Key Steps:**  
  - **Descriptive Statistics:** Summarized Age, Household Income, and Internet Usage using measures of central tendency (mean, median), dispersion (standard deviation, range), and shape (skewness, kurtosis).  
  - **Data Visualization:** Created histograms and boxplots to evaluate normality, detect skewness, and identify outliers.  
  - **Crosstabulation & Chi-Square Tests:** Assessed associations between categorical variables, including:  
    - Gender × Marital Status  
    - Region × Home Ownership  
    - Employment Status × Education Level  
    - Age Group × Having Children  
    - Political Affiliation × Region  
  - **Data Management:** Recoded Age into categorical groups (18–29, 30–44, 45–59, 60+) for more interpretable analysis.  
- **Skills Demonstrated:** Exploratory data analysis, statistical assumption testing, Chi-Square hypothesis testing, data recoding, interpretation of statistical output, and results reporting.
